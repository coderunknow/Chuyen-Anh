A. LISTENING (2.5 points)

Part 1: Listen to someone on the radio talking about how to improve your concentration. For questions 1-10, complete each sentence with NO MORE THAN THREE WORDS. (1.0 point)

Part 2: You will hear an interview with two trainee teachers, called Amy and John, about students using smartphones in school. For questions 11-15, choose the answer (A, B, C or D) which fits best according to what you hear. (0.5 points)

A. to be aware of advertising on smartphones

B. about the effects of overuse of smartphones

C. how to avoid being distracted by smartphones

D. to decide for themselves when to use smartphones

Part 3. You will hear a talk about One-Day Clean-Up. For questions 16-20, decide whether each of the statements is True or False. If it is True, put a tick (✓) in the box under T. If it is False, put a tick (✓) in the box under F. (0.5 points)
