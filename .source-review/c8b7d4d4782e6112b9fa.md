## Bộ đề thi nói Tiếng Anh chuyên vào lớp 10 Quảng Trị 2026–2027

Phần thi nói yêu cầu thí sinh thể hiện tư duy phản biện và khả năng ngôn ngữ linh hoạt thông qua 10 chủ đề mang tính thực tiễn cao:

#1. Talk about the most memorable experience in your life. Why is it the most memorable?

#2. Some tourists prefer visiting famous attractions, while others enjoy exploring less-known places. Which type of destination would you choose? Explain your reasons.

#3. Talk about city problems and suggest some solutions.

#4. Nowadays, many students spend little time doing physical exercise. What are the reasons for this problem, and how can students become more active?

#5. Talk about a person who has influenced you the most. Why do you admire him/her?

#6. Some students choose jobs based on high salary, while others choose jobs they are passionate about. Which do you think is more important? Explain your opinion.

#7. Nowadays, some natural wonders are being destroyed. In your opinion, what should people do to protect them?

#8. Many students today spend too much time using smartphones. Do you think smartphones are helpful or harmful to students? Explain your opinion.

#9. Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming more common in education and daily life. How can AI help students in their studies?

#10. Fast food is becoming increasingly popular among teenagers. What are the causes of this trend, and what effects can it have on young people's health?
